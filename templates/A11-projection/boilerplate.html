§ 05

Deviation verdict scale / z-score or robust band — never raw %

Use this scale when actual outcomes arrive and must be compared to the projection. Verdict is driven by z-score (or a robust band equivalent) — never by a raw percentage gap, which is mechanically unstable for near-zero or high-variance metrics.
